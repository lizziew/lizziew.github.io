# PlanForMe

An automated assistant that optimizes and organizes your schedule for
you.

It makes planning your day quick and easy, whether you have a lot of
overlapping events or just want to prioritize your meetings and
activities. You can:

  - Order your events by priority
  - Sort events into “Must do”, “Would like to do”, and “Don’t want to
    do” categories

PlanForMe:

  - Integrates with your calendars
  - Creates your plan for the day, taking into account each event’s
    category and priority

Just tap a button to add the plan to your calendar\!

Used Swift, Xcode, Firebase and EventKit.
